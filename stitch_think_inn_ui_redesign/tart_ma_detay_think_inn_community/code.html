Scalable LLM Architectures for Edge Computing

Dr. Marcus Chen

schedule Posted 4 hours ago • Principal AI Architect

The challenge of deploying Large Language Models (LLMs) on edge devices has historically been limited by memory bandwidth and computational power. However, with the emergence of 4-bit quantization and specialized NPU architectures, we are seeing a shift in what's possible at the periphery of the network.

Proposed Solution: Distributed Inference

We are currently investigating a 'Sharded Inference' model where a single 70B parameter model is logically split across a cluster of local edge nodes (e.g., smart gateways and specialized sensors). By utilizing a high-speed local mesh network, we can achieve latencies under 100ms for token generation, bypassing the need for cloud backhaul.

"The goal isn't just to make the model smaller, but to make the infrastructure smarter. We're moving from a hub-and-spoke AI model to a true neural mesh."

Current benchmarks show a 40% reduction in energy consumption compared to traditional cloud-relay methods. We are seeking collaborators with experience in FPGA-based acceleration and Zero-Knowledge proofs for decentralized model validation.
