Decentralized Neural Networks: Architectures, Challenges, and the Future of Edge-Native Intelligence

Authors: Dr. Elena Vaskov, Marcus J. Thorne, AI Research Collective

Publication: International Journal of Neural Systems (IJNS)

Date: October 14, 2024 • Citations: 42

Abstract

This paper introduces a novel framework for decentralized neural network (DNN) training and inference, specifically optimized for edge computing environments. As data privacy concerns grow and latency requirements become more stringent, the migration of computational intelligence from centralized cloud architectures to local node networks is imperative. We present a swarm-based optimization algorithm that minimizes communication overhead while maintaining global model convergence.

1. Introduction

The paradigm of centralized machine learning faces significant hurdles in the era of pervasive IoT. Large-scale data collection poses existential risks to consumer privacy and creates bottlenecks in transmission infrastructure. Decentralized architectures offer a promising alternative, yet they introduce significant complexities in synchronization and heterogeneous resource management.

"The future of intelligence is not in the monolith, but in the collective consensus of the edge."

2. Methodology

Our approach leverages a peer-to-peer (P2P) gossip protocol integrated with a localized gradient descent mechanism. Nodes participate in a weighted consensus model based on their hardware availability and data quality indicators. We utilize a 'Connectivity Node' model where each device acts as a functional hexagon in a larger mathematical grid of computation.
